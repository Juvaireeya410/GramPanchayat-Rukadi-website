Vision, Mission and Values

Vision and Mission

The approach of the Gram Panchayat is citizen-oriented, transparent and based on sustainable development. The main goal is to provide basic amenities and improve the quality of life of villagers.


Sanitation, Water Supply, Health, Education, Digital Services and Infrastructure Development are the key focus areas of Gram Panchayat Rukadi. Effective implementation of government schemes and continuous development of facilities are our priorities.

Default Values

  • Transparent and accountable administration
  • Citizen participation and trust
  • Balanced and inclusive development
  • Cleanliness, health and environment conservation
  • Strict adherence to government policies
  • Adoption of digital services
