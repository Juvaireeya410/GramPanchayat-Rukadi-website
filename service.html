Services provided through Gram Panchayat

Birth Registration & Certificate

If the birth occurs within the village limits, the birth is registered within 21 days and a birth certificate is issued.

Required Documents:

  • Application
  • Parents' Aadhaar Card
  • Report of ASHA / Anganwadi Worker
  • Hospital Documents as Required

Procedure:

Registration is done after confirmation that the birth took place within the village limits. Births occurring at a primary health center are registered at the same place.

Fee:

First Certificate Free
Then Rs. 20

Time:

7 Days

Death Registration & Certificate

If a death occurs within the village limits, the death is registered within 21 days and a death certificate is issued.

Required Documents:

  • Application
  • Aadhaar Card of Deceased Person
  • Hospital / Police Station Documents as Required

Procedure:

Registration is done after confirmation that the death occurred within the village limits.

Fee:

First Certificate Free
Then Rs. 20

Time:

7 Days

Marriage Registration & Certificate

Marriages are registered as per the Marriage Registration Act, 1998.

Required Documents:

  • Marriage Memorandum
  • Birth Certificates of Bride and Groom
  • Aadhaar Card / Voter ID / Driving License
  • Marriage Photographs

Procedure:

Marriage registration is completed after verification of the application and submitted documents.

Fee:

Rs. 50 – Rs. 200
Certificate Fee Rs. 20

Time:

-
